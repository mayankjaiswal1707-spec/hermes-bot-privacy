Hermes Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Hermes is a Telegram bot operated by its owner.

Information processed

Hermes processes messages, commands, and files you send to provide its requested functions. Telegram may also provide basic account metadata such as your Telegram user ID, username, and display name.

How information is used

Information is used only to respond to requests, operate the bot, maintain security, and improve reliability. Hermes does not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Storage and retention

Conversation data and operational logs may be retained only as long as reasonably necessary to operate, troubleshoot, secure, or improve the service. You may request deletion of data associated with your Telegram account by contacting the bot owner.

Third parties

Hermes relies on Telegram to deliver messages and may use service providers necessary to perform a requested task. Those providers handle information under their own policies.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect data, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

Hermes is not intended for children under the age at which they may independently consent to data processing in their jurisdiction.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the bot owner through Telegram. This policy may be updated when the service changes; the current version will remain available at this page.
